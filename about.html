About this site & how it works

Missoula Stump Guide is an independent information resource about stump grinding and removal for the Missoula, Montana area. Being clear about what this site is — and is not — matters.

What this site is

  • An independent publisher of general, practical information about stump work.
  • A place to learn about grinding vs. removal, cost factors, and the process.
  • A simple way to write down your project so your notes are on file.

What this site is not

  • It is not a tree-service or stump-grinding contractor.
  • It does not perform, bid, price, schedule, or dispatch any stump work.
  • It does not claim any contractor license or local contractor status.
  • It has no Missoula office or local street address and does not represent one.

What happens to a request

This site is new, and no provider is currently assigned to requests submitted here. When you send a request:

  • it goes only to the publisher and is kept on file;
  • it is not sold, rented, forwarded, or shared with a contractor;
  • you will not be enrolled in text messages or promotional email;
  • any reply, if any, is informational and does not mean the publisher will do the work.

If you need service soon, please contact a local provider directly. We will never imply that a contractor will call you when none is assigned.

Who runs it

Missoula Stump Guide is published by Jamison Worst as an individual publisher. It is an early, independent project. You can reach the publisher through the contact page, and you can read how your information is handled in the privacy notice.
