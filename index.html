Why this resource exists

Finding clear, non-salesy information about stump work can be surprisingly hard. Most search results are contractor sales pages. This site collects practical, general information so you can decide what you need, ask better questions, and recognize a fair scope of work when you talk to an independent local professional.

When you are ready, you can describe your stump project and we will keep your notes on file. We are transparent about what happens next: your request is held by the publisher and is not sent to any contractor.
