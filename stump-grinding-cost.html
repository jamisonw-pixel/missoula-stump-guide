What affects stump grinding cost

There is no single price for stump work. The best way to budget is to understand the factors a provider weighs when they quote. Here is what usually moves the number.

No quotes here. This page explains general pricing factors only. It is not a quote, estimate, or guarantee, and it does not state prices for any specific provider. Local pricing in the Missoula area varies by provider and by job.

The main factors

Stump diameter

Most providers think in terms of the stump's diameter at ground level. A wider stump takes more passes and more time, and many providers price at least partly per inch of diameter.

Number of stumps

Several stumps in one visit usually cost less per stump than a single-stump trip, because the provider only mobilizes equipment once. If you have more than one, group them into a single request.

Root spread and grinding depth

Grinding just below grade to replant grass is different from deep grinding for construction or a replacement tree. Surface roots that radiate out from the stump add area and time.

Access

A stump a machine can reach easily is cheaper than one behind a narrow gate, on a slope, near utilities or irrigation, or hemmed in by structures. Tell the provider about tight access up front.

Wood type and condition

Hard, dense, or green wood grinds more slowly than soft or well-rotted wood. Rocks and soil packed around the stump also dull equipment and slow the job.

Debris and cleanup

Grinding produces a pile of chips. Decide whether you want to keep them as mulch, spread them, or have them hauled away — haul-off and backfill add cost.

How providers commonly price

  • Per inch of stump diameter — a common baseline for grinding.
  • Per stump — sometimes used for uniform, easy-access stumps.
  • Hourly or per-job — more common for difficult access, large root systems, or full removal.
  • Minimum trip charge — many providers have a minimum that makes a single small stump cost more per inch than a batch.

These are common industry structures, not a specific offer. Ask each provider how they price and what is included (chip cleanup, backfill, surface-root grinding).

Questions worth asking

  • Is chip cleanup and haul-off included, or extra?
  • How deep will you grind — enough to replant grass, or deeper?
  • Will you grind surface roots, or only the main stump?
  • Do you fill and grade the hole for full removal?
  • Are you insured, and can you confirm the total before starting?
